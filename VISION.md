## Vision (draft, from his words above)
- The Governor's cockpit for the factory: see what needs him, the whole map at any zoom, and talk to the Mayor by text, voice, pictures and files.
- For him alone; it replaces an SSH session, from his phone.
- Most important: fast, never clunky, and a control that does what it says.
- Lines: his key stays on his phone; the VPS never sees plaintext; real sats are only his to move; a voice note is never sent to a third party.

- "there should be two kinds all right so for him I want him if possible his own user ... it's my Machinery that spins something up for him you know but everything else is his his settings is whatever though his mayor should you know by default work pretty closely to how you do you know but then he can tweak it"
- "if they have their own back end I don't know that I even have to license them you know they would do their own licensing"
- "my thought actually with poster is is that it would be free for people to use but if but that they would you know bring all their own accounts right I wouldn't Supply Claude for them"
- "a future Waypoint maybe you know where I can start selling full accounts to people ... I would provide the machinery and they would have to provide their own Claude"
- "there should be like this you know family plan type thing where it's like okay you can have a main license holder but then he you know is sharing his machinery and his his Claude with some number of other people but you try to keep things otherwise distinct"

The two kinds of user:
- The family plan: a main licence holder shares his machinery and build fuel with some number of other people, under his approval (each of a member's epics is released only on the holder's hands-on approval). Each member has his own login for his own Mayor, and otherwise keeps things distinct: his own Linux user, vault, beads, backend and settings. A member's Mayor works like the holder's by default; he can tweak it. (The split as settled at the end of the same Talk, turn 17-18, mw-6ww.108.)
- The free, bring-your-own kind: Postern is free to use; they bring their own backend, their own Claude and their own licensing. He supplies none of it.
- Later waypoint, not now: selling full accounts (he provides the machinery, they provide their own Claude).

The lines that do not move, for everyone:
- The holder's key stays on his phone.
- The VPS never sees plaintext.
- Real sats are only the holder's to move.
- A member's user never holds the holder's login or keys.
