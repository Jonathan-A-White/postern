# Demo: the gate, on your phone

1. On your Android phone, open https://postern.allmymind.org in Chrome.
2. You should see the Postern gate screen: "Postern" at the top, "The gate is locked" below it, and the app version underneath that.
3. Open Chrome's menu and choose "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app") to install Postern as a standalone PWA.
4. Open the installed Postern icon from your home screen — it should launch full-screen, without Chrome's address bar.
5. Turn on airplane mode (or otherwise disconnect from the network) and reopen Postern from your home screen — the gate screen should still load, served from the cache.
